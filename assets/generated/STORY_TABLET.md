# Open-ring tablet texture

Original project texture edited with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-21.
Input: `moon_tablet_albedo.png`. Output: `moon_tablet_open_ring.png`.
The old texture and GLB remain intact. The world overrides only the tablet front
material, preserving the side and foot materials and the original UV mapping.

Prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied game stone tablet albedo texture. Keep same square flat orthographic texture, same gray slate, cracks, lichens, pixel-art texel character, lunar crescent center, and lower rows of fantasy inscriptions. Change the upper emblem's TWO complete circular rings into visibly OPEN rings: erase an 80-degree wedge on the RIGHT centered slightly downward (roughly 4 o'clock), replacing that region with matching ordinary slate. The remaining ring ends must terminate cleanly at this gap, like an ancient intentionally open road emblem, not a broken random crack. Remove the right cardinal star/spike that falls in that gap. Keep top/left/bottom decorative stars. Directly below the emblem, abrade the FIRST row of runes into a visibly chiseled horizontal scar where a name was intentionally erased; preserve the two lower inscription rows. No new objects, perspective, text, borders, lighting or shadows. This will replace the albedo of an existing 3D stone tablet.

The output preserves the crescent and lower two rows, with an open right arc and
an abraded first inscription row. Generated gap orientation is approximate.
No third-party input was introduced.
